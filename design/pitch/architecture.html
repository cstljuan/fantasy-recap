Stat lines

One fantasy week for two rosters. The main game uses invented lines. Two week 9 games use real plays.

Scout, the research agent

agents/scout/   LangChain deepagents

A main agent plans and hands work to a stats researcher, a film room and a fact checker. Out: player profiles and 28 scouting reports.

Service and database

server/, db/

Node service. Pushes a game as rows, keeps opt-ins, answers momentum, trends and top plays.

Tiger Data (PostgreSQL with TimescaleDB). Every play is a row in a hypertable, with continuous aggregates over it.
