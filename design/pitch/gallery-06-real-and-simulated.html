06 / Honest scoreboard

What is real.
What is not.

The scorekeeper does not lie about the score

Real

  • The rules engine, and a check that replays every game and compares every number.
  • The player names and rosters.
  • 3,177 real plays, and two week 9 games built from them.
  • Season tendencies read from Sleeper, cross-checked against ESPN.
  • About 1,020 recorded lines for the two announcers.

Simulated

  • Every play of the main football game and of the soccer match: invented stat lines, real names.
  • The league, the standings and the second manager.
  • The week of waiting. The demo is one viewer on one device.

Not checked

  • Sound and speed on a real phone.
  • The database beyond its schema and saving and reading plays.
  • A model run for 27 of the 28 scouted players.
