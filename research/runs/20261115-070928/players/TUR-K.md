### Background

- Cameron Lawrence Dicker, nicknamed "Dicker the Kicker", was born May 6, 2000. Wikipedia says he was born in Hong Kong to Kelly and Rachel Dicker and is the only NFL player born there. The data file (ESPN and Sleeper pulls) lists his birthplace as Austin, TX. The sources disagree; the data file value is Austin, TX and the Wikipedia value is Hong Kong. A search summary of a Shanghai American School alumni profile and an SCMP headline point toward Hong Kong, but those pages were not opened. (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cameron_Dicker; date: date not shown; sources disagree on birthplace)
- His parents were from Seattle but were working in China. He spent his first 11 years in Shanghai, grew up watching soccer and is a Liverpool F.C. fan, and the family moved to Austin, Texas in 2011. (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cameron_Dicker; date: date not shown; from Wikipedia)
- He did not play football until fifth grade, in an expat youth league in Shanghai. At Lake Travis High School in Austin he was a two-time all-state selection, kicker for three seasons and punter for two, won the 2016 Texas 6A Division I state title and set a school record with a 53-yard field goal. (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cameron_Dicker; date: date not shown; from Wikipedia)
- He played at Texas, where he started as a freshman, became known as "Dicker the Kicker" after a 40-yard game-winner against Oklahoma, and holds the school record for field goals made. (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cameron_Dicker; date: date not shown; from Wikipedia)
- He signed with the Rams as an undrafted free agent in 2022 and has also been with the Ravens and Eagles. Wikipedia calls him the most accurate kicker in NFL history by field goal percentage (92.8%), a figure as of that page's text, not recomputed here.
