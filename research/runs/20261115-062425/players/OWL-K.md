## Family and background

### Background

- Born January 29, 1994 in Kailua, Hawaii, to John and Rochelle Fairbairn; his full given name is John Christian Ka'iminoeauloameka'ikeokekumupa'a Fairbairn and he has gone by Ka'imi since childhood. In Hawaiian the name means 'seeker of wisdom'. (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ka%CA%BBimi_Fairbairn; date: date not shown; from Wikipedia)
- At Punahou School he was a two-sport star in football and soccer (two-time first-team all-state central defender, two state titles); he tried water polo first, nearly drowned, and went back to football. Teammate DeForest Buckner; ranked the No. 4 kicking recruit nationally. (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ka%CA%BBimi_Fairbairn; date: date not shown; from Wikipedia)
- At UCLA he won the kicking job as a true freshman in 2012, was the first Bruin to score 100 points in each of four seasons, was a consensus first-team All-American as a senior and won the 2015 Lou Groza Award. He became the Pac-12 career scoring record holder. (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ka%CA%BBimi_Fairbairn; date: date not shown; from Wikipedia)
- Signed with Houston as an undrafted free agent in 2016 and became the starting kicker in 2017. Career field goal rate of 87.9 percent, called the fourth most accurate kicker in NFL history. (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ka%CA%BBimi_Fairbairn; date: date not shown; from Wikipedia)
