## Family and background

### Background

- Cameron Lawrence Dicker was born May 6, 2000, in Hong Kong, to Kelly and Rachel Dicker, and is the only NFL player born in Hong Kong. (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cameron_Dicker; date: date not shown; from Wikipedia)
- He spent his first 11 years in Shanghai, where his father worked, then moved to Austin, Texas in 2011. He grew up watching soccer and is a Liverpool F.C. fan. (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cameron_Dicker; date: date not shown; from Wikipedia)
- He did not play football until fifth grade, in an expat youth league in Shanghai. At Lake Travis High School he was a two-time all-state pick, kicker for three seasons and punter for two, and won the 2016 Texas 6A Division I state title. (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cameron_Dicker; date: date not shown; from Wikipedia)
- At Texas he was All-Big 12 as a kicker and punter, holds the school record for field goals made, and became known as 'Dicker the Kicker' after a 40-yard game-winner against Oklahoma in the Red River Showdown. (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cameron_Dicker; date: date not shown; from Wikipedia)
- He signed with the Rams as an undrafted free agent in 2022 and was also with the Ravens and Eagles before settling with the Chargers. (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cameron_Dicker; date: date not shown; from Wikipedia)
- Listed at 6 ft 1 in, 216 lb, age 26, jersey 11, Chargers kicker (depth chart K1), fifth NFL season, Madden rating 82. (source: research/data/TUR-K.json (bio); date: 2026-11-15; from the data file)
- Career regular season: 144 of 155 field goals (92.9 percent), 22 makes from 50 to 59 yards, 136 of 140 extra points, 70 games. Wikipedia calls him the most accurate kicker in NFL history by field goal percentage. (source: research/data/TUR-K.json (career); date: 2026-11-15; from the data file)
