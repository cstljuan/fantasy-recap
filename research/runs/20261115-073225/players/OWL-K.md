### Background

- Full name John Christian Kaʻiminoeauloamekaʻikeokekumupaʻa Fairbairn, born January 29, 1994 in Kailua, Hawaii; he has gone by Kaʻimi since childhood, a Hawaiian name that means 'seeker of wisdom'. (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ka%CA%BBimi_Fairbairn; date: date not shown; from Wikipedia)
- He attended Punahou School, where he was a two-sport star in football and soccer (central defender, two-time first-team all-state, two state titles), and was teammates with DeForest Buckner. He was ranked the No. 4 kicker recruit nationally. (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ka%CA%BBimi_Fairbairn; date: date not shown; from Wikipedia)
- At UCLA he won the kicking job as a true freshman in 2012, was the first Bruin with at least 100 points in each of four seasons, became the Pac-12 career scoring record holder, and was a consensus first-team All-American and Lou Groza Award winner in 2015.
