## Family and background

### Background

- Born January 7, 1997, in Pompano Beach, Florida; he grew up in the Golden Acres public housing project there. (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lamar_Jackson; date: date not shown; from Wikipedia)
- He played in the same South Florida youth league as future teammate Marquise Brown and, at age 11, won the local youth Super Bowl as quarterback, safety and kicker. (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lamar_Jackson; date: date not shown; from Wikipedia)
- He attended Boynton Beach High School, then played at Louisville, where he won the 2016 Heisman Trophy, Maxwell Award and Walter Camp Award. (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lamar_Jackson; date: date not shown; from Wikipedia)
- Baltimore took him with the last pick of the first round (32nd overall) in the 2018 draft. (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lamar_Jackson; date: date not shown; from Wikipedia)
- He became the starter as a rookie after Joe Flacco was hurt, and was the youngest quarterback to start a playoff game at age 21. (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lamar_Jackson; date: date not shown; from Wikipedia)
- He is a two-time NFL MVP (2019 and 2023) and the all-time leader in quarterback rushing yards. (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lamar_Jackson; date: date not shown; from Wikipedia)
- He is 6 ft 2 in, 205 lb and in his ninth NFL season at age 29. (source: https://www.espn.com/nfl/player/_/id/3916387/lamar-jackson; date: 2026-11-15; one source)

### Family
