## Family and background

### Background

- Saquon Rasul Quevis Barkley was born February 9, 1997 in the Bronx, New York, and the family moved to the Lehigh Valley in Pennsylvania in 2001; they settled in Coplay in 2005. (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saquon_Barkley; date: date not shown; from Wikipedia)
- At Whitehall High School he rushed for 3,646 yards and 63 touchdowns from sophomore to senior year, was co-winner of Mr. Pennsylvania Football in 2014, and also lettered in basketball and track (100 meters in 10.90 seconds). (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saquon_Barkley; date: date not shown; from Wikipedia)
- At Penn State he set school records for career all-purpose yards (5,538) and rushing touchdowns (43), finished fourth in the 2017 Heisman voting, and left after three seasons. (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saquon_Barkley; date: date not shown; from Wikipedia)
- The Giants drafted him second overall in 2018; he was Offensive Rookie of the Year, went to the Pro Bowl in 2018 and 2022, and signed a three-year deal with the Eagles after the 2023 season. (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saquon_Barkley; date: date not shown; from Wikipedia)
- In 2024 he ran for over 2,000 yards (2,005 in the data file) with Philadelphia, helped win Super Bowl LIX, and was named AP Offensive Player of the Year. (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saquon_Barkley; date: date not shown; confirmed by two sources (Wikipedia and research/data/GOB-RB.json career by_season))
