### Background

- Born November 22, 1999; grew up in Fort Morgan, Colorado, and attended Fort Morgan High School, where he played baseball, basketball and football. (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trey_McBride; date: date not shown; from Wikipedia)
