### Background

- Cameron Lawrence Dicker was born May 6, 2000, in Hong Kong, and is nicknamed 'Dicker the Kicker'. He is a placekicker for the Los Angeles Chargers and, per Wikipedia, the only NFL player born in Hong Kong. (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cameron_Dicker; date: date not shown; from Wikipedia (section stored in the data file))
- He lived in Shanghai for his first 11 years, moved to Austin, Texas in 2011, and did not play football until fifth grade in an expat youth league. At Lake Travis High School he was a two-time all-state kicker, and the team won the 2016 Texas 6A Division I title. (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cameron_Dicker; date: date not shown; from Wikipedia)
- He kicked at Texas, where he started as a freshman, earned All-Big 12 honors, and holds the school record for field goals made. His college long was 57 yards (2019 vs Rice). (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cameron_Dicker; date: date not shown; from Wikipedia)
- The Rams signed him as an undrafted free agent in 2022.
