### Background

- Born January 30, 2002 in Tucson, Arizona. He attended Salpointe Catholic High School, set a state record with 7,036 rushing yards and 114 touchdowns, and as a senior was the Arizona Gatorade Football Player of the Year (2,235 yards and 38 touchdowns on 126 carries). (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bijan_Robinson; date: date not shown; from Wikipedia)
- A five-star recruit, he went to Texas, was Alamo Bowl MVP in 2020 (10 rushes for 183 yards and a touchdown), ran for 1,127 yards in 2021 and 1,580 yards with 18 touchdowns in 2022, when he was a unanimous All-American and won the Doak Walker Award. (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bijan_Robinson; date: date not shown; from Wikipedia)
